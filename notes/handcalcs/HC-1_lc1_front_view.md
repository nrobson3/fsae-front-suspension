# HC-1: LC1 (3 g bump) in the front view

_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** an independent 2D check of the 3D force solver. If your pushrod force lands within a few per cent of the 3D value, the 6x6 solve is right.

**Given** (front view, y outboard and z up, mm; the left front corner at static ride height):

| Point | y | z |
|---|---|---|
| UCA_O | 559.6 | 276.0 |
| LCA_O | 577.3 | 132.0 |
| PR_O | 545.5 | 163.8 |
| PR_I | 268.2 | 440.7 |
| UCA pivot trace | 208.8 | 196.6 |
| LCA pivot trace | 195.0 | 104.6 |
| Contact patch | 610.0 | 0.0 |

Vertical tyre load Fz = 2006 N up at the contact patch (3 x the static corner load). The upper wishbone is one two-force member along UCA_O -> its pivot trace; the lower wishbone likewise, and the pushrod acts on the lower node along PR_O -> PR_I. The tie rod carries nothing in a pure vertical case.

**Find:** (1) the upper-wishbone force, (2) the lower ball-joint force on the upright, (3) the lower-wishbone and pushrod forces.

**Method:** free body of the upright, moments about the lower ball joint; then force balance; then the lower node (two unknowns, two equations). Draw the free bodies to scale first.
