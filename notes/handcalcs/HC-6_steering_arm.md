# HC-6: Steering arm, beam theory and where it breaks

_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** DV-2. The plan wanted the FEA within 10 % of a hand calc at a plain section; the arm has none. This packet shows why, then checks the root properly.

**Given:**

- LC6 (driver effort, wheels blocked) tie-rod force on the arm: 2094 N across the arm and 9 N along it (+ towards the housing).
- Two ears, each 6 mm thick and 32 mm deep; the bolt hole is 8.1 mm.
- Section S at 10.9 mm from the bolt. The upper ear (TR1, filleted R2, which the FEA covers) meets the body 17.8 mm from its bolt; its width is 32 mm.

**Find:** (1) the beam stress at both edges of section S (sigma = -M y / I - P / A); (2) compare with the FEA there (answer key) and explain the gap (how far is S from the load, compared with the depth?); (3) the nominal stress at the root and Kt for the R2 fillet (Peterson, flat bar with shoulder fillets in bending); (4) compare the peak with the FEA's steering-arm root peak.
