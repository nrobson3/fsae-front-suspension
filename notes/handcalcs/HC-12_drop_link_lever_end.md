# HC-12: The drop link's lever end and the ARB lever

_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** requirements S-20 and S-21. The drop link's lower rod end is bolted through the ARB lever's blade along the bar's axis, beside the blade on a misalignment spacer, with a second spacer and a 24 mm washer outside it (R-08: single shear, captured). The bolt is loaded as a cantilever; the link pulls beside the blade, so the blade bends and twists.

**Given:**

- The ARB's largest torque 162.0 N m (warp, stiff blade; `vehicle_derived.json`). Lever: the link's moment arm about the bar 120.0 mm (link along ARB_L -> ARB_R).
- Bolt M8 10.9 (Fty 940 MPa), stress area 36.6 mm^2; blade face to the ball's centre 11 mm (half the 12 mm ball + a 5 mm spacer).
- Blade 4130N (Fty 435 MPa), 8 x 25 mm; hub outer radius 18.6 mm; the ball's centre 15 mm beside the blade's mid-plane.

**Find:** (1) the drop link's force; (2) the bolt's bending stress at the blade face on the stress area's diameter, its direct shear, von Mises and the FoS; (3) the blade's bending stress at the hub, its torsion from the offset (rectangle: T (3a + 1.8b) / (a^2 b^2)), von Mises and the FoS; (4) the end's shear-out past the M8 hole (two planes, 0.577 Fty).
