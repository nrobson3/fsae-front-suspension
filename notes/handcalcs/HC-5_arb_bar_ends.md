# HC-5: Anti-roll bar, body and splined ends

_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** requirement S-09. Since D26 the bar sets the ARB's rate: three solid bars, swapped to adjust, between two stiff levers. The stiff bar carries the largest torque; its ends are splined into the levers.

**Given:**

- Worst bar torque T = 162 N m (warp: one wheel at full bump, the other at the droop stop; stiff setting).
- Stiff bar: solid 4340 heat treated to 180 ksi (Fty 1124 MPa, shear yield 0.577 x Fty), body 13.9 mm.
- Ends upset to 25 mm and splined, minor diameter 23 mm; Kt = 3 on the minor diameter (assumed, the high end of the splined-shaft range).

**Find:** (1) the body's torsional shear and FoS; (2) the end's shear at the minor diameter with Kt, and its FoS; (3) the smallest minor diameter for FoS 1.5, and why the ends are upset above the body.

**Fatigue (S-23):** the bar reverses every left-right corner. Given the largest cornering roll's bar torque, 69 N m, fully reversed; Ftu 1241 MPa; the body and its fillets ground, the spline machined; an R4 fillet where the body meets the 25 mm upset.

**Find:** (4) the torsional endurance limit with Marin's factors (surface, size, load) at the body and at the spline's minor diameter; (5) Kt at the shoulder (Peterson's torsion chart for a stepped round bar) and the infinite-life FoS at the shoulder and at the spline, taking Kf = Kt.
