# HC-9: Member-end welds and rod-end threads in fatigue

_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** requirement S-07b. Every link force passes through a fillet weld at each tube end (to the ball-joint node or the threaded insert) and through the rod end's first engaged thread.

**Given:**

- Member forces in every duty state from the 6 x 6 solve at static ride height (the duty states of `data/duty_loads.json`), and the duty cycles between them (114,000 cycles over the life).
- Weld: IIW, load-carrying fillet weld, toe FAT 63 on the tube's nominal stress, root FAT 36 on the throat (throat = tube wall, a drawing rule), slope 3, no endurance limit.
- Thread: M8 x 1.25 shank, stress area 36.6 mm^2, EN 1993-1-9 detail 50, slope 3; no credit for the jam nut. Engagement 1.5 d in a 4130 insert.

| Member | Tube section [mm^2] | Largest force range [N] | Its cycles |
|---|---|---|---|
| UCA front leg | 41.9 | 1543 | 8,000 |
| UCA rear leg | 41.9 | 1342 | 8,000 |
| LCA front leg | 50.7 | 3202 | 8,000 |
| LCA rear leg | 50.7 | 3034 | 8,000 |
| Tie rod | 57.2 | 879 | 10,435 |
| Pushrod | 50.7 | 1266 | 10,435 |

**Find:** (1) the stress range at the toe, on the throat and in the thread for each cycle; (2) Miner's damage on each S-N line and the factor on stress that brings it to 1; (3) the insert thread's stripping load (0.577 Fty over half the cylinder at the thread's major diameter) against the member's largest static force.
