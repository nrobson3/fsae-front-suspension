# HC-11: Chassis tabs and brackets

_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** requirement S-19. Each inboard joint is held by a pair of 4130N plates (or one bracket web) welded to a chassis tube (`scripts/chassis_tabs.py`); the joint's force bends each plate in its own plane about the weld.

**Given** (per part; plates are 4130N, the weld-affected yield 348 MPa = 435 x 0.8):

| Part | Plates | t [mm] | Width [mm] | Bolt to weld [mm] | Force [N] | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upper wishbone front tab | 2 | 2.5 | 22 | 22.9 | 1984 | UCA front leg, largest force, along the leg |
| Upper wishbone rear tab | 2 | 2.5 | 22 | 22.8 | 2406 | UCA rear leg, largest force, along the leg |
| Lower wishbone front tab | 2 | 2.5 | 40 | 24.5 | 4329 | LCA front leg, largest force, along the leg |
| Lower wishbone rear tab | 2 | 2.5 | 40 | 24.4 | 5467 | LCA rear leg, largest force, along the leg |
| Rocker pivot bracket | 2 | 4 | 32 | 43.4 | 1730 | rocker pivot reaction, LC1 with the bump stop |
| Damper chassis tab root | 2 | 4 | 36 | 55.3 | 2204 | damper force, LC1 with the bump stop |
| Damper chassis tab | 2 | 4 | 16 | 30.0 | 2204 | damper force, LC1 with the bump stop |
| Rack bracket web | 1 | 4 | 30 | 27.3 | 2094 | LC6 rack force along the rack, all on one bracket |

**Find:** for each part, split the force into its parts along and across the plate; the root bending 6 F_across L / (n t w^2) and the direct stress F_along / (n t w); the FoS.
