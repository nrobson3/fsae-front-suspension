_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** requirement R-06 (FSAE V.3.2.5: under 7 deg at the steering wheel).

**Given:**

- Rack: 75 mm of rack per steering-wheel turn (`vehicle.json`; the Kaz rack itself is 119.6 mm). Lash after adjustment 0.1 mm at the rack (assumed).
- 2 PTFE-lined rod ends per tie rod, 0.025 mm radial play each (assumed).
- The column, its two universal joints and the quick release: 2 deg allowed (chassis side).

**Find:** the play at the rack, taken to the steering wheel, plus the column's share; compare with the rule.
