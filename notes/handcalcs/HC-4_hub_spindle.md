# HC-4: Hub spindle in rotating bending

_Hardpoints v1.7. Inputs are the current design's; generated by `scripts/handcalcs.py`. Work it on paper first, then check `answer_key.md`._

**Why:** requirement S-15. The spindle turns under a bending moment that is fixed in space, so every wheel revolution is one fully reversed stress cycle.

**Given:**

- Hollow 7075 spindle at the bearing seats: 40 mm outside, 26 mm bore.
- The critical section is the shoulder at the outboard cone's back face, 15.5 mm outboard of the wheel-centre plane; the shoulder is 46 mm across with a 1 mm fillet (the cone's back-face chamfer caps it).
- Tyre forces act at the contact patch, in the wheel-centre plane, R = 200 mm below the axis.
- Load cases (`data/loadcases.csv`) and the duty spectrum (`data/duty_loads.json`: each state's tyre forces; `vehicle.json` duty: each state's share of the 3000 km life; outside and inside wheel split evenly).
- S-N line: the upright's construction (`materials.json`) with this shaft's own size factor kb = 1.24 d^-0.107: S = 408 MPa x (N / 1e3)^(-0.1203). Design yield 391 MPa.
- The key takes each load through the force solver's own load points (static camber, the pneumatic trail, the wheel centre or the kerb point), which moves the moments by about 1 % from the 2D formula below.

**Find:** (1) the section modulus; (2) the bending moment at the shoulder for each load case (M_x = -a Fz + R Fy, M_z = a Fx) and the static FoS; (3) Kt for the shoulder fillet from Peterson's chart; (4) the revolutions in each duty state and their stress amplitude; (5) Miner's damage and the factor on stress that brings it to 1 (the fatigue FoS). (6) Repeat (3)-(5) for a 3 mm fillet behind a shoulder ring.

**Method:** the brake torque does not pass this section (the disc and wheel bolt to the flange outboard of it). Mean stress is zero (rotating bending).
