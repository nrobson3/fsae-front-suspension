Basis: Events per km for a tight FSAE autocross / endurance course (about 20 direction changes and 8 braking zones per km; 10 bumps or kerb strikes at 2 g). Corner events are shared among the cornering states (including braking-while-cornering) by their distance share, brake events among the braking states, and every corner is counted as a full outside-to-inside reversal (a slalom), which is conservative: a corner followed by a straight is a smaller cycle. Brake and bump events cycle against the 1 g static state.
