## Cup fit in the 7075 upright

- **Housing bore P6, cups normal class:** 13-45 µm interference at 20 °C.
- **Running hot (+60 °C):** aluminium grows away from the steel cup by 49 µm, so the fit becomes -36 to -4 µm (negative = clearance). A press fit alone cannot hold the cups hot: the interference needed (about 59 µm) would roughly double the hoop stress below.
- **Hoop stress at the largest interference** (Lamé, 9 mm housing wall): interface pressure 7.0 MPa, **hoop stress 30 MPa**. Sustained tensile stress in 7075 should stay low for stress-corrosion; T7351 temper and this level are acceptable.
- **So:** P6 on the 68 mm cup seats (13-45 um interference at 20 C), plus a bearing-retaining compound (Loctite 641); cups pressed in from each side against a central rib in the upright bore (back-to-back pair: both cups are loaded towards the centre); cones clamped by the axle nut, which sets a light preload. Spindle: m6 on the 40 mm hub spindle (rotating inner-ring load).
