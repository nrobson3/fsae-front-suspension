# Upright FEA (hardpoints v1.7)

Generated by `scripts/fea/upright_fea.py`. Do not edit by hand. gmsh + scikit-fem, quadratic tetrahedra; 7075-T7351, design yield 391 MPa (0.9 x typical 434, standing in for A-basis). Geometry: `cad/upright/upright_L_fea.step` (the one-piece upright, v4).

**Root fillets in the analysed geometry:** caliper lugs 8 mm; post 8 mm; lower web 8 mm; upper clevis ear 1 3 mm; upper clevis ear 2 3 mm; lower ball-joint ear 1 SHARP; lower ball-joint ear 2 2 mm; steering-arm ear 1 2 mm; steering-arm ear 2 SHARP.

Base mesh: 961,794 degrees of freedom, refined round 16 hot spots found by a coarse pre-pass over every load case. 24 elements with a scaled Jacobian below 0.05 after curving had their mid-side nodes put back on straight edges (worst afterwards 0.20); the nearest is 45.4 mm from the governing peak. Loads as they act (pin-bearing pressure in the ear and lug holes, cosine pressure on the cup seats tilted onto each row's pressure centre, thrust on the rib); every face a case can load (each hole, both cup seats, both rib faces) area-checked on this mesh, within 5 % of its drawing value; a minimal 3-2-1 support. Applied loads balance to a moment residual of at most 0.17 % of (largest load x 100 mm) (the run stops above 1 %); the largest support reaction is 1.34 % of the largest load.

## Validation of the method (S-06c)

The upright has no section slender enough for beam theory, so the method is validated where beam theory holds: a 7075 bar 160 x 32 x 6 mm (the steering-arm ear's section, made long), clamped at one end and loaded through an 8.1 mm pin hole by 1000 N across and 300 N along it, with the same mesher, pin-bearing load and integration-point stress as the upright (`benchmark_lug.py`). Normal stress at mid-span, 3 mm band at each edge:

|---|---|---|---|
| +14.0 | -57.57 | -57.55 | +0.03 % |

## Fatigue (Goodman)

Se = 95 MPa: 150 MPa at 5e8 cycles x ka (machined, Shigley's fit) x kb 0.9 x ke 0.814 (99 % reliability). Amplitude = von Mises of half the stress-tensor range, mean = signed von Mises of the mean; Goodman line to the design Ftu 453 MPa; target FoS 1.5. "Static" is LC1 / 3 (LC1 is a 3 g bump at +30 mm of travel).

- cornering LC2 <-> LC2i: FoS **1.12** at [30.8, 578.6, 184.9] mm
- braking LC3 <-> static: FoS **1.36** at [-11.1, 593.9, 115.0] mm
- at the hole edges (pin contact, for information): cornering LC2 <-> LC2i FoS 1.50
- at the hole edges (pin contact, for information): braking LC3 <-> static FoS 2.29

This is a screen: it asks for unlimited life with every cycle at the peak load, which 7075 cannot give, so it falls short of 1.5 (deviation DV-3, S-07a). The fatigue check is Miner's rule over the duty spectrum in `notes/upright_fatigue.md` (S-07c).
