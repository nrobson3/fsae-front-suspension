# Design FMEA and test plan (hardpoints v1.7)

Generated by `scripts/dfmea.py` from `data/dfmea.json` (the ratings and controls); the numbers in the text come from the generated matrix and results. Do not edit by hand. The script refuses to write unless every requirement that is not passing, and every fastener-sweep warning, has a row or a test that says what would close it.

RPN = severity x occurrence x detection, before and after the recommended actions (the after ratings assume the actions are done). Scales (anchor points; values between them sit between):

- **Severity:** 10 a wheel loses its location, or the brakes or steering fail, without warning; 9 the same with warning first (play, noise), or the car fails a rule at technical inspection; 8 steering or braking clearly degraded; 7 a sudden handling change the driver may not catch; 5 performance degraded, the car still drives safely; 3 a small loss the driver may not notice; 1 no effect.
- **Occurrence:** 10 it is the design: it happens; 6 margin under 1.2, an assumed input not yet confirmed, or not analysed; 4 margin near 1.2 to 1.5, or inputs that are estimates; 3 margin of 1.5 to 2, or a method with one benchmark; 2 margin of 2 or more by a benchmarked method; 1 prevented by design with a large margin.
- **Detection:** 9 no check; 7 analysis with assumed inputs; 5 analysis only, by a benchmarked method; 3 a planned inspection on the built car catches it; 2 a planned test on the built car catches it; 1 measured on the part itself before release.
