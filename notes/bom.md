## How the design quantities were found

- **Upright blank:** STEP envelope 79.5 x 148.5 x 213.8 mm, + 3 mm a side -> 3.5 in plate, 155 x 220 mm, 8.48 kg of 7075 for a 0.78 kg part (about 11 to 1 buy-to-fly; normal for a 3+2-axis machined upright).
- **Rod ends:** 10 per corner (the rod-end rows of `data/misalignment.csv`: wishbones 4, tie rod 2, pushrod 2, drop link 2). Each takes one M8 10.9 bolt (length from the bolt schedule in `notes/fasteners.md`), an all-metal lock nut (nylon inserts are limited to 80 C) and a jam nut on its shank (R-10); the drop link's lever end also takes two 24 mm washers (R-08). Bolts into parts are locked by a screw-locking Heli-Coil (the lower ball joint) or drilled heads wired in pairs (the caliper and rack bracket bolts) (R-09).
- **Tube:** member length from `data/member_checks.csv` less the two rod-end shanks, + 50 mm cut allowance per tube: 15.875 x 0.889 mm 2.7 ft, 19.05 x 0.889 mm 4.0 ft, 15.875 x 1.245 mm 1.2 ft.

## Findings from pricing

- **Rod-end part number corrected.** The data said Aurora `AM-M8`, which does not exist. The 33135 N rating the checks use belongs to the **AM-M8T** (PTFE lined, heat-treated alloy steel, 15.88 mm ball; Timken item page). `data/members.csv` and `data/joints_misalignment.csv` now name the AM-M8T; no FoS changes.
- **Cost-down option (not taken):** the plain Aurora MM-M8 (13591 N ultimate, 36.96 USD) against the highest rod-end load in the six checked members (5467 N, LCA rear leg) gives FoS 2.49 on S-03's >= 2.25 target (passes), saving about 806 USD a corner. Not taken: it has an unlined steel-on-steel race (greased, wears and gains play) and a low-carbon body, and the drop-link loads are not in the force solver. Worth a quote on the AM-M8T first.
- **Spring rate (settled 2026-11-04):** the spring had been picked as 25 N/mm (143 lb/in) from an assumed 2.5 N/mm metric list, softer than anything sold for the TTX25: Ohlins and Hyperco both start at 150 lb/in. `vehicle.json` now holds the real lb/in steps, so the calcs pick 150 lb/in (26.3 N/mm, a Hyperco FSAE spring): 2.91 Hz against the 2.66-2.94 Hz window, and the loads, FEA and fatigue were re-run on it.
- **Biggest lines:** wheel, tyre, damper and rod ends are 78% of the estimated corner. The 3 in 7075-T7351 plate needs a quote (only T651 is listed retail).
